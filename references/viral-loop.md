# Viral Particle-Earth Loop

Use this reference to keep the first release narrow, legible, and shareable.

## Default Product Promise

`把你的一刻，放进艺术史。`

The first visitor promise is not “browse an art-history database.” It is “touch a striking world, make it respond to you, and leave with a result that belongs to you.”

## P0 Journey

1. **Immediate Earth**
   - Render the real particle Earth on entry.
   - Show one short instruction.
   - Make touch, drag, and press available without login.

2. **One Gesture**
   - Pull a wide field of particles with the finger.
   - Keep the globe silhouette smooth at rest.
   - Give the release a decisive gather, burst, and return.

3. **Personalize After the Wow**
   - Ask for one image and one optional short line.
   - Keep city optional unless location is essential to the point.
   - Avoid a multi-step profile or metadata form.

4. **Private Preview**
   - Play the personal transformation immediately for the uploader.
   - Mark it clearly as private and not uploaded to the public world.
   - Allow an immediate local poster download without moderation.

5. **Request Public Publishing**
   - Ask for an explicit publish action and rights/display consent.
   - Start moderation only after that action.

6. **Approved Public Artifact**
   - After approval, create the public moment and share artifact.
   - Open public video download, share, and referral only now.

7. **Referral**
   - Deep-link to the approved point.
   - Reveal the moment quickly.
   - Offer `接上这条轨迹` as the single next action.

## Four-Second Share Sequence

- `0.0–0.7s`: whole particle Earth resolves from darkness;
- `0.7–1.6s`: the user's point pulls the surrounding field;
- `1.6–2.8s`: particles gather into the approved image fragment;
- `2.8–4.0s`: the fragment bursts and returns to the globe, leaving the point.

Keep UI chrome out of the export. Use small project identity and one short action line. The thumbnail should show the Earth at or near the peak transformation, not a settings screen.

## Virality Rules

- Recognition: every artifact must still look like the same product.
- Ownership: one meaningful element must come from the user.
- Evidence: the world must visibly retain the approved point.
- Portability: the result must work outside the H5.
- Referral: the shared link must reopen the user's state.
- Low friction: effect first, upload second, login only when durable identity is required.

## P1 and P2

After P0 is proven:

- P1: show a restrained set of recent approved public points;
- P1: let visitors open another person's moment;
- P2: let a visitor continue one approved point with a new point;
- P2: notify the original participant that their trajectory was continued.

Do not add realtime multiplayer until asynchronous continuation is understood and used.

## Measurement

Instrument named events rather than generic clicks:

- `particle_play_started`
- `first_gesture_completed`
- `personalize_opened`
- `upload_private_preview_ready`
- `private_poster_downloaded`
- `public_publish_requested`
- `moderation_approved`
- `share_artifact_ready`
- `share_action`
- `referred_point_opened`
- `trajectory_continued`
- `experience_replayed`

Review completion and referral behavior before adding more spectacle.
